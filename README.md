# How Long Can a Canadian Spousal Sponsorship Application Take

## Introduction

For couples planning to build their future together in Canada, spousal sponsorship can be an important pathway to permanent residence. However, one question often comes up before the application even begins: how long will the process take?

The answer depends on several factors, including whether the sponsored spouse lives in Canada or abroad, where the couple plans to settle, whether the application is complete, and how long background and eligibility checks take. Couples planning to live in Quebec may also face additional provincial procedures that affect their overall timeline.

Waiting for an immigration decision can be challenging, particularly when partners are living in different countries or trying to plan employment, housing, travel, and family responsibilities. Understanding the process can help couples prepare realistic expectations and avoid mistakes that could add unnecessary delays.

This guide explains Canadian spousal sponsorship processing times, the difference between inland and outland applications, Quebec-specific considerations, and practical steps couples can take while waiting. It is written for people across Canada, including French-speaking couples in Quebec, while keeping the information in English.

For general immigration information and assistance, you can also visit [Asra Immigration](https://asraimmigration.ca/).

## How Long Does Spousal Sponsorship Take in Canada?

There is no single processing time that applies to every spousal sponsorship application. Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) updates its estimates as application volumes, available processing capacity, and other circumstances change.

As a general planning reference, estimates reported in October 2026 put applications for spouses or common-law partners living outside Quebec at approximately **18 months for outland applications and 26 months for inland applications**. For applicants intending to settle in Quebec, reported estimates have been considerably longer, at approximately 32 to 33 months, depending on the application route.

These figures are estimates, not promises. They may change, and individual applications can be finalized sooner or take longer. Before making important decisions, check the official [IRCC processing-time tool](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times) for the latest estimate for your specific application category.

It is also important to understand that processing time does not necessarily include every practical step a couple may need to complete before submitting an application. Obtaining civil documents, arranging translations, correcting errors, and preparing relationship evidence can all take additional time.

## What Is the Difference Between Inland and Outland Sponsorship?

One of the first decisions couples need to understand is whether their circumstances fit the in-Canada process or the Family Class process commonly associated with outland sponsorship.

### Inland spousal sponsorship

An inland application generally refers to a couple applying through the Spouse or Common-Law Partner in Canada Class. The sponsored spouse or partner lives in Canada and must meet the relevant requirements for this class.

This route may be suitable for couples who are already living together in Canada and want to pursue permanent residence while remaining in the country. However, applying inland does not automatically grant the sponsored person legal status, permission to work, or permission to remain in Canada beyond the validity of their existing immigration status.

Eligible applicants may be able to apply for an open work permit under the applicable rules while their permanent residence application is being processed. Eligibility conditions apply, so couples should review the current IRCC guidance rather than assuming every applicant qualifies.

### Outland spousal sponsorship

Outland sponsorship generally refers to applying through the Family Class when the sponsored spouse or partner may be living outside Canada. This route can also be relevant to some couples where the sponsored person is already in Canada but their circumstances make the Family Class route appropriate.

It can be useful when partners are living in different countries or when the sponsored spouse needs to travel internationally. However, travel and entry to Canada remain subject to the applicable immigration rules. A pending sponsorship application does not guarantee a visitor visa or admission at the border.

Neither route is automatically faster for every couple. The right choice depends on eligibility, location, travel plans, family circumstances, and the latest processing estimates.

## Why Can Spousal Sponsorship Take Longer Than Expected?

Even when a couple submits a complete application, several factors can affect the timeline.

### 1. Incomplete applications

Missing forms, unsigned declarations, unclear scans, or absent supporting documents can cause problems at the beginning of the process. An incomplete application may be returned rather than processed, potentially forcing the couple to correct the issues and submit again.

Before applying, review the current IRCC checklist carefully. Check every form, confirm that required signatures are present, and ensure that documents are readable.

### 2. Additional relationship evidence

IRCC assesses whether a relationship meets the requirements for sponsorship. Officers may request additional information if the submitted evidence does not adequately explain the couple's circumstances.

For example, partners who have lived apart for long periods may need to provide context about their communication, visits, and future plans. Couples who have recently married may need to demonstrate the development and genuine nature of their relationship.

A request for additional documents does not automatically mean that an application will be refused. However, responding accurately and within the stated deadline is important.

### 3. Background and admissibility checks

Background checks, police certificates, medical examinations, and other admissibility requirements can affect how long a file takes to finalize.

Some checks involve information from multiple countries or government authorities. If records are difficult to verify, additional review may be necessary.

Applicants should provide truthful and complete information about their personal history, travel, previous immigration applications, and other matters requested by IRCC.

### 4. Different processing workloads

IRCC processes a large number of family sponsorship applications. The time required can vary with application volumes, available resources, and the number of cases already waiting for a decision.

This is one reason why a processing estimate viewed several months ago may no longer reflect the current situation.

### 5. Delayed responses from applicants

If IRCC requests a document, explanation, medical examination, or other action, the applicant should follow the instructions and deadline provided.

Waiting too long to respond can slow the case and may have more serious consequences if a deadline is missed. Keep contact information current and monitor the online account and email regularly.

## Does Spousal Sponsorship Take Longer in Quebec?

For couples planning to settle in Quebec, this is a particularly important consideration.

Quebec has its own immigration selection responsibilities and provincial undertaking procedures for certain family sponsorship cases. As a result, family sponsorship applications intended for Quebec can take longer than comparable applications for other provinces.

The federal and provincial stages are related, but they are not interchangeable. Applicants may need to complete a Quebec undertaking process after receiving instructions from IRCC. The timing and eligibility requirements depend on the circumstances and current provincial rules.

**Do not submit a Quebec undertaking application before you are instructed to do so.** IRCC advises applicants to wait for the relevant instructions because submitting it too early can prevent the province from processing it.

French-speaking couples living in Montreal, Quebec City, Laval, Gatineau, or other Quebec communities should therefore consider the full process when planning their future together. It is sensible to allow flexibility when making decisions about leases, work, travel, and family arrangements.

For current requirements, consult the official [Government of Quebec immigration website](https://www.quebec.ca/en/immigration).

## When Does the Processing Clock Start?

IRCC generally measures processing time from when it receives a complete application to when it makes a decision. The exact starting point depends on how the application is submitted.

For online applications, the processing period generally starts when the application is submitted. For mailed applications, it generally starts when the application arrives at the appropriate mailroom.

This distinction matters because couples may spend weeks or months preparing documents before submission. That preparation time is separate from the processing estimate published by IRCC.

After the application is received and checked for completeness, IRCC generally sends an acknowledgement of receipt. Keep this confirmation and any application number in a safe place, as they can be useful when checking progress or responding to requests.

## Can Couples Speed Up a Spousal Sponsorship Application?

There is no guaranteed method to make a standard spousal sponsorship application move faster. Paying a representative, contacting IRCC repeatedly, or submitting unnecessary documents does not automatically shorten the queue.

However, couples can take practical steps to reduce avoidable delays.

First, prepare a complete application using the latest official forms and checklist. Second, provide clear, consistent evidence of the relationship and ensure that names, dates, addresses, and personal histories match across documents. Third, arrange translations that comply with IRCC requirements. Fourth, respond promptly to requests for additional information. Finally, keep contact details current and check the application status regularly.

If an important circumstance changes, such as a new address, a change in family composition, or a change in contact information, follow IRCC's instructions for updating the application.

If an application appears to be taking longer than the published estimate, check the official processing-time information and review any messages in the online account. Contacting IRCC may be appropriate when there is a specific issue to resolve, but routine inquiries generally do not accelerate processing.

## Can a Sponsored Spouse Work While Waiting for Permanent Residence?

Some sponsored spouses or partners in Canada may qualify for an open work permit while their permanent residence application is being processed. Eligibility depends on the applicable rules and the applicant's circumstances.

A pending sponsorship application by itself should not be treated as permission to work. Applicants must have the appropriate authorization before beginning employment.

Similarly, couples should not assume that a permanent residence application automatically extends temporary status. A person in Canada needs to understand their own status and apply for any necessary extension or permit under the relevant instructions.

For the most reliable guidance, review the current information on the [IRCC spousal sponsorship program page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/family-sponsorship/spouse-partner-children).

## What Should Couples Do While Waiting?

The waiting period can be a good opportunity to keep records organized and prepare for the next stage of life in Canada.

Couples can save copies of their submitted forms, supporting documents, payment receipts, and IRCC correspondence. They should also keep evidence of any significant changes in their relationship or living arrangements where relevant.

If the partners are living apart, they can maintain ordinary records of communication and visits. They should not manufacture evidence or create documents solely to make the relationship appear stronger. Any information submitted to immigration authorities must be truthful.

It is also wise to plan finances realistically. Processing times may change, and couples should avoid making irreversible decisions based on an assumed approval date.

If a sponsored spouse is outside Canada, the couple should plan travel carefully and understand that a pending permanent residence application does not guarantee permission to enter Canada. If the spouse is in Canada, they should monitor their temporary status and work authorization separately from the sponsorship process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is spousal sponsorship in Canada completed in 12 months?

Not necessarily. Twelve months has been an important service standard for certain overseas family sponsorship applications outside Quebec, but actual processing times can be longer. Current estimates vary by application category and destination. Always check the official IRCC tool before planning around a specific timeline.

### Is inland sponsorship faster than outland sponsorship?

Not in every case. Processing times change, and the appropriate route depends on the couple's circumstances. Compare current estimates for both relevant categories and confirm that you meet the eligibility requirements before choosing a route.

### Why does sponsorship take longer in Quebec?

Quebec has additional provincial procedures and its own immigration selection responsibilities for certain family sponsorship cases. These steps can increase the overall timeline compared with applications destined for other provinces.

### Can I visit my spouse while the application is processing?

Possibly, but a pending sponsorship application does not guarantee a visitor visa or entry to Canada. The person travelling must meet the relevant temporary-entry requirements and should be prepared to explain their circumstances truthfully.

### Will hiring an immigration consultant make my application faster?

No consultant can guarantee faster processing or influence the final decision. Professional support may help reduce avoidable errors, but the application remains subject to IRCC's processing system and applicable requirements.

### How can I check my application status?

Follow the instructions provided by IRCC and use the appropriate online status or processing-time tools. Keep your application number and correspondence available, and respond to any requests by the stated deadline.
